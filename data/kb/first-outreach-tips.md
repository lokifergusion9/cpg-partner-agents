title: Writing a first outreach email to a broker or distributor

A first message to someone who does not know you works best when it is short, specific and easy to answer.

- **Say who you are in one line:** the brand, the product and where it is already sold.
- **Say why them:** one concrete reason this broker or distributor fits, such as the region they cover or a category they already carry. Do not claim a relationship you do not have.
- **Make one clear ask:** a 15-minute call, or permission to send samples and a price sheet.
- **Keep it under about 150 words,** with a plain subject line that matches the content.
- **Be honest about numbers:** state only sales and facts you can back up.
- **Identify the sender and offer an easy way out,** such as a line saying they can reply "no thanks" and you will not write again.

Follow up once after a week or so, not more. Brokers and distributors answer people who make their job easier, so attach the price sheet and item details when asked rather than in the first message.
