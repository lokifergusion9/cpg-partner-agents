Trade spend is the money a brand pays to the trade (distributors and retailers) to support sales, as opposed to money spent on advertising to consumers. For many CPG brands it is one of the largest costs after the product itself.

Common forms:

- **Off-invoice discounts:** a lower price on an order, often for a set period.
- **Scan-based or "scan-down" allowances:** the discount is paid on units actually sold to shoppers.
- **Promotional features:** payments for ad placement, displays at the end of an aisle or a spot in a circular.
- **Slotting and listing fees** for getting onto the shelf.
- **Free fill or free goods** for a first order.

Trade spend is usually planned as a percentage of sales in a yearly budget and is negotiated with each account. The risk for a small brand is overspending on discounts that pull sales forward without building repeat buyers. A sound habit is to track, for every promotion, the extra units sold against what the promotion cost.
