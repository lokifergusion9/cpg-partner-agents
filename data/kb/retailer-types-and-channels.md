Different retail formats buy differently, so a pitch should match the format.

- **Conventional supermarkets and grocery chains:** large assortments, a formal buyer for each category and set review dates. They value steady supply, promotion support and brands with a record of sales.
- **Natural and specialty grocers:** focused on organic, allergen-friendly or local products. Often more open to new and small brands and to a strong brand story.
- **Mass merchants and club stores:** very high volume, tough pricing and strict supply requirements, usually a later step for a growing brand.
- **Convenience stores:** small shelf space, fast-moving single-serve items, frequently supplied by distributors.
- **Independent grocers:** buy through wholesalers and distributors and decide fast, one store at a time.
- **Online retailers and marketplaces:** need good product data and photos, and shipping that fits the product.

A common path is to prove the product in a few independent or specialty stores, build local sales data and then use it to approach larger regional chains.
